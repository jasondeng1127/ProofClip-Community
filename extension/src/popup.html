Feature guide

Explore what ProofClip can do.

Capture

Three capture modes and shortcuts

Capture the full page body, a text selection, or a visible region with one click. Shortcuts: Alt+3 = Body, Alt+2 = Region, Alt+1 = Selection.

Read mode and long pages

Page bodies are cleaned automatically (ads, navigation, comments and cookie banners removed), and the full captured text stays in your local Archive. Notion delivery is sent in ordered batches; if a service limit is reached, ProofClip reports the delivery failure instead of silently shortening the record.

Region screenshots

Region captures are watermarked with the capture time and source URL. Right-click cancels a selection, just like Esc.

Capture route

Choose Save locally (the default) or Send to Notion direct mode. An empty selection is refused, and Recent capture previews your latest capture.

Right-click selection

Select text on a page, open the page context menu, then choose the ProofClip selection command. It uses your current capture route.

Duplicate capture warning

When you capture a recently saved page again, ProofClip asks whether to Continue or Cancel before creating another record. If you do not choose, capture continues after a short countdown.

Archive

Local archive and search

Every capture is saved in your browser (IndexedDB) and can be searched by title, source, body, note or tag.

Filters

Filter the archive by project, tag, capture mode and delivery status.

Projects

Create, rename and delete projects; deleting a project moves its cards to Unfiled after confirmation.

Card management

Edit a card's project, tags and notes in place, and read the full body text with its own scroll area.

Send to Notion

Send one card at a time through your chosen template, or use direct capture. A region direct-send delivers only the screenshot with Source, Captured and Mode. After a local save, the page toast offers Edit (opens that card in Archive) and Send to Notion.

Send filtered records

Use Send filtered to Notion to send the pending cards that match your current Archive filters. Sent and failed cards are left unchanged.

Outbox recovery

A failed delivery stays in Outbox. Use Retry for a retryable delivery; use Resend after verification after you reconnect Notion or correct its target and mapping.

Direct delivery

A successful Send to Notion direct capture goes to your chosen Notion Data Source and is not added to the local Archive. If it fails, its record stays in Outbox for recovery.

Remove a local copy

Remove one local card and its paired Outbox item without touching the Notion page already sent.

Export and clear

Export JSON is a read-only archive that includes screenshots. Clear local data removes the archive, settings and Outbox.

Settings

Notion connection

Connect through Notion's own approval page; no token is stored in this extension. Disconnect removes the server-side connection.

Delivery target

Connect Notion once, choose a Data Source, then select Set up ProofClip. ProofClip adds and maps its standard fields automatically, including Tags and Note. No token, deployment, or manual property setup is needed. If you choose another Data Source later, run Set up ProofClip for that Data Source. Use Advanced mapping only when an existing Data Source needs a custom override.

Template editor

Create, edit, delete and set a default browser-only template. New templates start with the standard field set; Title and URL cannot be removed, and deleting a template never changes archived evidence cards.
