# Security policy

Do not report a suspected secret exposure, OAuth weakness, token-vault problem, cross-extension CORS issue, or data-loss risk in a public issue. Until a private reporting address is published, contact the maintainer through the repository's private security-reporting channel after the repository is made public.

Reports should include affected version, self-hosted deployment steps, expected and actual behavior, and minimal redacted evidence. Never include Notion access tokens, client secrets, vault keys, full captures, or personal workspace data.
