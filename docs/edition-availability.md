# ProofClip edition availability

This page distinguishes the public Community baseline from the future Commercial edition so a visitor can decide what is usable today and what is not.

## Community edition: available now

ProofClip Community is the public, AGPL-3.0-only, self-hosted baseline in this repository. You can inspect the source, deploy your own Cloudflare Worker and D1 database, connect a Notion integration that you control, and load the extension from source. The deployment operator is responsible for configuration, upgrades, backups, and operations.

Start with [Getting started](getting-started.md) and then follow the [deployment guide](../deploy/README.md).

## Commercial edition: not available yet

The Commercial edition is not currently released or deliverable. The Google account required for its release and distribution is currently suspended, and an appeal is in progress. Restoring that account is a release dependency, so no Commercial delivery date can be committed until the appeal is resolved and account access is restored.

This is a status statement, not a pre-order or availability promise. A new public update will be published after the release dependency is cleared.

## How the Commercial edition is intended to be delivered after release

When the Commercial edition becomes available, it is intended to provide a cloud-managed service with a more complete feature set, a more polished experience, and continuing version updates. Users will not need to deploy a Worker, D1 database, or Notion OAuth infrastructure. The intended path is download, one-click initialization, and use; connecting a user's own Notion workspace remains an explicit authorization step.

Until that release is available, use Community only if self-deployment and self-operation are acceptable to you.
